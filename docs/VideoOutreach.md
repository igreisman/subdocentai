## Suggested email text for the button / comment route

> Hi, I'm a volunteer docent aboard USS Pampanito in San Francisco. We've embedded your
> video [title] on our educational site, SubmarineDocent.org, with full credit and a link
> to your channel: https://submarinedocent.org/web/videos.html. If you'd like anything
> changed, or would rather we not feature it, just let me know. Thanks for making it.
